Design system · logo

The logo

A capability's logo is a picture on a 64px tile with its name written under it. The artwork is drawn once per capability, from one subject and two colours, and carries no lettering. The shell applies the rest: the corner, the shadow, the size and the label. So only the artwork differs between two capabilities, and a logo keeps it for life.

Four real logos on the wallpaper, at the size the desk draws them.

The artwork arrives as a full-bleed square, and the shell clips the corner, casts the shadow and sets the size, with no line around it. The tile is the only square on the surface with a rounded corner.

Tile64 pxOne size on the desk. Pixels, so the artwork never scales with text.
Corner10 %A percentage, so the shape holds at any size.
EdgeNoneThe artwork's colour field is the silhouette.
Shadow3 px / 4 pxInk at 24%. Hard offset, never blurred.
ArtworkFull bleedEdge to edge, with no frame or inner margin.
Label gap7 pxBetween the tile and the name under it.

The desk always draws 64px. Other sizes show the corner and the artwork holding their shape; the shadow stays 3px across and 4px down at any size.

A logo wears two colours: a ground and a companion, the colour its object is drawn in. They come from two different hue families, and each family opens onto four shades. The capability's seed picks which shade of each family it wears, so two capabilities that name the same family need not look alike. Signal is never a logo colour.

The name is written straight onto the desk in light type over a soft halo, with no plate behind it. It is the one blurred type treatment in Aluna, and the prompt notice shares it because it too is read off the wallpaper.

Type a long name to see the two-line clamp. The logo grid stands on the sky, so the sky is the ground to check after any change to the wallpaper or the halo.

Pass Drop shadow What it does
Edge 0 1px 1px ink Gives each glyph its own edge, so the letterform stays crisp
Near 0 1px 2px ink at 40% Carries the reading on an ordinary ground
Deep 0 2px 4px ink at 15% Shows over pale ground, where the logo grid stands

The passes are chained filters, so each one also shades the passes before it. The prompt notice wears a text-shadow form of it, with two wider passes around the words.

Until its artwork lands a capability stands on a placeholder: diagonal bands of ground-deep on surface-2. The bands crawl while a picture is on its way, through the build and then the logo request, and rest when none is coming.
