Design system

The desk, the window, the line, the palette and the type.

A capability in Aluna is an app on a desk: a logo on the ground, and one window that holds everything it shows. Every boundary is drawn by hand in one ink, every corner you can press is mitred, and every fill comes from one closed palette.

Everything here is live and runs on the shipped stylesheets in design/styles/. Form controls are on the controls page and the capability logo on the logo page.

Press a logo to open its capability and another to swap what the window holds. Drag the window by its title bar and resize it from the bottom-right corner. Type into the prompt bar and press Make it to grow one. The capabilities are fixtures, and a build runs on a timer, ending the way picked below.

Unfocused, the title bar desaturates to 50% and its title drops to 72%; the frame and content stay as they are. Anatomy names each part and what it is made of. Each window carries its own seed, so no two frames on a desk are the same.

A line that encloses is drawn and a line that divides stays true. A part is drawn when its whole perimeter completes a cycle of its hand: a 22px checkbox, 88px around, is drawn. Only what stands on the desk casts a shadow: the window, the prompt bar and the logo tile, and the question panel laid over a window's body.

Flat fills at high chroma, with no shading. Depth comes from ordered bands, light in front and dark behind, never from a gradient or a blur. A colour that covers a large area is held back, and the same colour in a small area runs at full strength. The palette is closed: a screen uses no colour that is not a token. A logo's artwork is the one exception, with shades of its own on the logo page.

Structure: four fills and the ink

Ink draws every line and sets type, and is never a solid fill. Labels on the two dark fills and the names on the desk are set in surface.

Ink at reading strengths

ink-2 reads on the light fills: surface, surface-2 and the title-bar panes. ink-3 reads on the two window fills only.

Tint anchors

Eight named colours, and a generated screen may wear any of them. Shade carries surface type and the others carry ink, except teal and violet, which carry no readable label and so are never buttons. Violet is the focus ring.

Reserved

There is no dark theme. The palette is daylight and ignores the viewer's colour-scheme preference.

Two variable faces, both OFL and self-hosted: Fraunces for display and window titles, Outfit for everything a capability generates. No monospace face appears in the product outside the developer panel. Sizes are in rem, so the reader's text size grows the layout with the text; the pixel sizes on these pages are what they come to at the default.

The shell draws these for every capability, so generation never decides them. They run the same code as the desk. A record opens in place of the collection, under a back control, and the create action opens the same form empty.

The collection · feed

Search and the create action share the first row, and under 620px of window each takes a row of its own. The count sits below them, directly above the first record. Type in the search: a filtered count states how many matched and how many there are, and a search that matches nothing says so beside the total.

The collection · grid

Only the records change. Feed and grid are the two layouts.

The record form

Label in small caps above the control, guidance below, short fields paired two-up. A rule separates the actions: Save and Cancel on the left, Delete on the right. A new record has no Delete.

The records linking here

Each relation with records pointing at this one gets a section between the fields and the actions, headed with the linking capability's label and a count. The cards are that capability's own, in its order, and a press opens the record there, asking first when the form has unsaved changes. Five show, and Show all adds the rest. A section never collapses, Save never posts it, and the actions stay the last row. A new record has none.

The record's bar

The bar holds only Back, with no title. Back names where the record was opened from, a record by its title and a list by its label, and returns there. A record opened from a pasted address, after a reload or from the bare desk names its own list. Save, Cancel and Delete leave the same way.
